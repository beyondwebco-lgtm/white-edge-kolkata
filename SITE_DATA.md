## 5. Standard Turnkey Workflow (6 Steps)

1. **Step 01 – Consultation**: Understand the brand, site, budget, architectural requirements, and visibility goals.
2. **Step 02 – Site Survey**: Inspect the location, measure exact dimensions, and review structural and electrical conditions.
3. **Step 03 – Design & Planning**: Develop photorealistic 3D concepts, CAD layouts, material selections, and project BOQs.
4. **Step 04 – Manufacturing**: Fabricate using approved materials, CNC/laser cutters, and experienced metal/acrylic craftsmen.
5. **Step 05 – Installation**: Complete safe, professional, and accurately aligned on-site installation with certified riggers.
6. **Step 06 – Support & Maintenance**: Provide preventive maintenance, LED module replacements, and warranty service.
